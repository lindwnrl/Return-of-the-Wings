## Structure

The page is a lit specimen cabinet. Every section is a case: hairline deep-forest frame with
corner ticks, a case number in the frame's corner margin (never as an eyebrow above a heading),
one specimen (photograph or drawn plate), and a museum label card with dotted-leader metadata rows.

Ground: Natural Cream. Cabinet darkens once, for Threats (deep-forest case, cream text) — that
contrast is the page's only ground change and marks the turn in the story.
